# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **`sonido pedal controls [EFFECT]`** prints how to play the pedal as a card: its face, every footswitch press per T2 position, the LEDs, the knob pages, the jam's setup page and pick zones, each jam effect's and factory rig's K1–K6, the rig list and power-on gestures; with an effect, its knobs' travel and noon. It is drawn from `sonido_patch::controls`, the functions the firmware's control tick dispatches through, so it can't drift from the pedal.
- **One level contract** (`sonido_core::level`): at its defaults every effect plays at the loudness of its input, and its mix control does not change that loudness. Every kernel declares `const LEVEL: Level` (a trim and, with a mix control, the measured wet/dry correlation) from a generated table, `crates/sonido-effects/src/calibration.rs`, and mixes with `Self::blend` / `Self::blend_stereo`: a sine/cosine constant-power law normalized for that correlation (polynomial sine, bit-trick inverse square root, no transcendentals on the M7), with a finite slope at both ends of the knob. Effects without a mix control get the trim on their output from the adapter. `cargo run -p sonido-effects --example calibrate --release` regenerates the table from a synthetic strummed guitar peaking at −20 dBFS (the measured median of a humbucker into the Hothouse; `sonido_analysis::signals::reference_guitar`) by integrated LUFS; `tests/level_contract.rs` holds every effect within 0.5 dB fully wet and 1.5 dB across its mix. Looper and tuner are uncalibrated utilities
- **The pedal is a rig player**: `sonido_pedal` plays every sound through `sonido_patch::PatchPlayer` with registry-built effects, as the graph-player plugin does. Its rig list is the jam (three slots picked with its knobs, saved as you play), up to 15 rigs in the QSPI rig bank, and the factory rigs; it powers on into the last rig played. On a rig the knobs are its six macros; footswitch holds step through rigs, FS1 taps bypass and FS2 taps tempo (the control rule, under Changed). Rigs are checked against the CPU and memory rule before they replace the playing one, and LED 2 blinks the rig's position or the refusal's code
- **Pedal state in flash**: the active rig, the jam (A and B), its topology and settings (`JamSettings`) and the tapped tempo are written to a state sector (`sonido_patch::pedal::PedalState`) once nothing has changed for 2 s
- **USB rig link** (`sonido_patch::link`): while it plays, the pedal is a USB serial device that lists, reads, writes (after checking), erases and activates bank rigs, reads the jam back as a rig, reports status and CPU load, and restarts into its bootloader for a firmware update (`Request::Bootloader`)
- **Host side of the rig link** (`sonido_patch::client`): `LinkClient`, generic over any `Read + Write` stream; `send_rig` replaces the bank rig with the same name, otherwise takes the first empty slot; `enter_bootloader`; `open_pedal` / `pedal_ports` behind the `serial` feature find the pedal by its USB id `1209:0001`. Tested against an in-memory pedal
- **`sonido pedal`** (CLI): `info`, `list`, `push`, `pull`, `erase`, `play` and `update`, with a global `--port`. `push` takes a `.json` or `.bin` rig, a preset `.toml` or `--from-dsl`, checks it with `validate_for_daisy` and sends it by the name rule (or `--slot`); `pull` saves a bank rig or the jam as editor JSON; `update` requests the bootloader and flashes a firmware `.bin` with `dfu-util`
- **`sonido_patch::dfu`**: firmware updates through `dfu-util`, moved from `sonido-gui` and now firmware only. It accepts only the Daisy bootloader (QSPI region `0x90000000`, firmware at `0x90040000`), not the STM32 ROM bootloader
- **`validate_for_daisy`** (`sonido_patch::validate`): the one pedal check, used by the editor's export, the CLI (`patch export --pedal`, `pedal push`) and the factory-rig tests
- **Editor rig name**: a **Name** field at the top of the Export menu, saved in the session (`Session::name`, default "My Rig"); it names the CLAP export, the pedal's bank rig and the graph player's rig
- **The jam's setup page** (T2 in the middle): K1 input, K2 morph time, K3 master, and K4–K6 pick slot 1–3's effect by zone (fully left empty, then `JAM_EFFECT_IDS`). A pick loads once its knob rests 0.3 s, starts at the effect's voiced defaults, and is refused if it would break the CPU rule (LED 2 blinks 4). Input, master and morph time are saved (`JamSettings`, in the jam patch's globals)
- **Tap tempo sets the jam's delay**: in the jam, an FS2 tap makes each delay's time one beat, in A and B
- **Factory rigs** (`crates/sonido-rigs`): six rigs (Shimmer Cathedral, Fuzz Lab, Space Echo, Granular Drift, Harmonic Habitat, Amp Stack), one set for the pedal (encoded into the firmware image by its `build.rs`) and the graph player's rig library, beside the rigs the editor exports. K1 input, K2 morph time, K3 master around a level-matching trim generated by `cargo run -p sonido-rigs --example calibrate --release`, K4–K6 each rig's own controls; `tests/factory.rs` holds each at the dry guitar's loudness (±0.5 dB at the middle of its morph, ±3 dB at A and B), within the pedal's budget, and through a binary round trip
- **`Effect::snap_params` and `PatchPlayer::apply_controls_now`**: a freshly built rig starts at its values instead of smoothing there from the effects' defaults
- **`ParamDescriptor::with_default`** builder
- **`scripts/update_cycle_table.py`** regenerates the pedal's cycle table from a `bench_kernels` log; `bench_kernels` gained a rig-path pass (each effect as the registry builds it, with the pedal's control tick)
- **Tests**: `level_contract.rs` and `reverb_decay.rs` (sonido-effects), `knob_calibration.rs` (sonido-platform: each jam effect at noon within 1.5 dB of the dry guitar, every knob's extremes usable), `factory.rs` (sonido-rigs)
- **All 36 effects ship as plugins** (was 20): amp, cabinet, de-esser, drone, glitch, looper, multiband compressor, pitch shift, plate reverb, shelving EQ, spring reverb, stereo widener, texture, time stretch, transient shaper and tuner join. `tests/plugin_inventory.rs` fails if the plugin catalog and the registry ever diverge
- **Sonido design system** ([DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md)): tokens retuned to contrast targets (labels 8:1, values 16:1), six effect families with their own phosphor, the Sonido Vector display face, neon primitives, an LED-ring knob, selectors and meters. Every plugin now draws the same **cabinet**: marquee, a synthwave screen with a live scope of the plugin's own input and output, IN/OUT meters with peak hold and a clip latch, the control deck, and a status line. Bypass shows INSERT COIN
- **CRT glass**: the plugin's OpenGL bridge renders through a post-process pass (mipmap bloom; inside the screen, phosphor persistence, curvature, aberration, scanlines, aperture grille, vignette), switchable from the footer
- **`sonido-preview`**: opens any plugin's editor in a window with a test signal, and saves PNG snapshots (`--snapshot`); `graph-player --demo-rig` previews the graph player
- **Graph player editor**: the graph player has a cabinet (RIG family) showing the loaded rig's chain, a RIG browser, the six macros, morph and gains. Choosing a rig asks the host to restart the plugin, which rebuilds it off the audio thread
- **Rig library folder**: one conversion (`sonido_config::preset_to_patch`) serves the CLI and the plugin, and one folder (`sonido_config::paths::rig_library_dir`, now with macOS and Windows locations) serves the editor's Export ▸ CLAP and the graph player
- **Measured Cortex-M7 costs for all 36 effects**, on a Daisy Seed at 480 MHz over SWD (BENCHMARKS.md), replacing the ×25 desktop estimates as the source for pedal budgets. `bench_kernels` now covers all 36 kernels (was 19), warms up, and reports the mean and worst of 128 blocks instead of one cold block, for the bare kernel and on the rig path; the cycle table holds the rig-path worst blocks. The estimate model, checked against the first run's bare-kernel means: median +14%, 28 of 36 within its ±50%
- **`all_effects` benchmark**: every registered effect at defaults through the plugin path (was 19 of 36); results for all 36 are in BENCHMARKS.md
- **Time Stretch Sync** works: it was a parameter nothing read. With Sync on, grain length snaps to the beat subdivision nearest Grain Size at the host tempo
- **Macro auto-assign skips Mix and Output**, as documented: `ParamFlags::MACRO_EXCLUDE` is now set on them (it was set nowhere)
- **One parameter-name and value rule** (`ParamDescriptor::matches_key` / `parse_value`): name, short name or string id, ignoring case, spaces, dashes and underscores; values like `-6dB`, `1.2kHz`, `1.5s`, `50%`, `Hard Clip`. The registry, preset loader, validator, CLI and plugin text entry all use it
- **Pedal CPU load meter**: `sonido_pedal` times every audio callback with the DWT cycle counter and logs each second's mean, peak and over-budget count, with the playing rig (or the jam's slots and routing) and the input and output levels, over defmt RTT; USB status reports the same load. The whole pedal is measured in a Hothouse under guitar (EMBEDDED.md): the jam distortion → reverb → ringmod runs at 74.7% mean and 84–85% peak with no callback over budget and no overrun at boot, from a 463.1 KB image (of 480 KB)
- **VST3 wrapper shims**: `scripts/bundle-vst3.sh` packages a [clap-wrapper](https://github.com/free-audio/clap-wrapper) shim per CLAP plugin (`dist-vst3/`, `make plugins-vst3`); each `.vst3` loads the `.clap` of the same name from the standard CLAP install locations. Release artifacts now ship a `vst3/` directory beside `clap/` on all targets. Wrapped plugins pass Steinberg's VST3 validator (47/47)

### Changed
- **Pedal LEDs**: each now shows what the footswitch below it does. LED 1 (FS1, on/off) is lit while on and blinks the rig's number or an error code; LED 2 (FS2, time) flashes the tempo, or with T2 in the middle shows the morph (dark at A, lit at B, flickering while gliding). The output-level meter is gone.
- **Defaults are the voiced sweet spots**, and a knob at noon selects them: the pedal's noon table (`sonido_platform::noon`, `sonido_daisy::noon_presets`) is gone, so a pedal at noon and a freshly opened plugin start identical. `adc_to_param_biased` puts the default at noon even at a range end (the near-extreme linear fallback is gone) and splits STEPPED options around it; new knob floors start the filter cutoff at 150 Hz and the bitcrusher at 5 bits. Changed defaults (descriptor and `Params::default`): bitcrusher downsample 1→4; delay time 300→380 ms, feedback 40→35%, mix 50→35%, feedback LP 20000→6000 Hz, feedback HP 20→80 Hz; distortion drive 8→26 dB, tone 0→1.5 dB; filter cutoff 1000→2200 Hz, resonance 0.707→3.0; flanger depth 35→50%; gate attack 1→5 ms; phaser rate 0.3→0.45 Hz, depth 50→70%, stages 6→4, feedback 50→30%, min freq 200→300 Hz, max freq 4000→3000 Hz; preamp gain 0→15 dB; reverb room 50→60%, decay 50→55%, damping 50→45%, pre-delay 10→20 ms, mix 50→35%, ER level 50→40%; ringmod frequency 220→300 Hz, depth 50→100%; stage Haas side R→L; vibrato depth 100→50%. Golden files regenerated
- **Kernels mix through the level contract** (`Self::blend`) instead of `wet_dry_mix`; the linear `sonido_core::wet_dry_mix` remains for the graph engine's bypass fade. The `defaults_produce_near_unity_gain` test is superseded by `level_contract`
- **One CPU rule for export and pedal**: a rig's measured rig-path worst blocks plus `NODE_OVERHEAD_CYCLES` (400) per node within `CHAIN_FRACTION` (80%) of the block (`cycle_table::chain_fits`), used by the editor's export check and the CLI (`validate_for_daisy`), the pedal's rig check and the jam's pick knobs. The cycle table was re-measured on a Daisy Seed (2026-10-22) on the rig path
- **The pedal builds effects through the registry** (`Adapter<K, SmoothedPolicy>`, as the plugins do) instead of unsmoothed `Adapter<K, DirectPolicy>` wrappers, so a rig sounds the same on the pedal as in a DAW. `DirectPolicy` remains for single-kernel firmware examples
- **One control rule for the pedal** (ADR-032), the same in every rig and T2 position. Footswitches: FS1 tap is on/off, and bypass now crossfades to dry (5 ms) while the rig keeps running (it copied input to output); FS2 tap is tap tempo, or with T2 in the middle a glide between A and B (a tap mid-glide turns back); either held 0.4 s changes rig right then, while held (FS1 back, FS2 forward). T2 is up A, middle Morph, down B (B was the middle). Knobs: the top row K1 K2 K3 is levels with K3 always a volume, the bottom row K4 K5 K6 the sound. Every `knob_map` was reordered (K1 an extra control, K2 mix, K3 output, K4–K6 the three main controls), and so were the factory rigs' macros. The jam's input and master apply in every T2 position (master was Morph only). The bootloader is a power-on gesture (both footswitches) or a USB request (`sonido pedal update`); FS1 held at power-on still starts the jam
- **`PEDAL_EFFECT_IDS` is now `sonido_registry::JAM_EFFECT_IDS`**, reordered for the pick knobs (distortion, bitcrusher, filter, ringmod, chorus, phaser, delay, reverb) and no longer append-only: jams are stored by effect UID
- **`sonido patch export`** reads a `.json` or `.bin` rig as well as a preset `.toml` or `--from-dsl`, and writes `.json` or `.bin` by the output's extension. `--pedal` now means the rig runs on the pedal (`validate_for_daisy`), not that it uses only jam effects. `patch inspect` reads `.json` too
- **The editor sends rigs over USB**: "Flash to pedal (DFU)" is now **Send to pedal**, which sends the rig to the playing pedal with `send_rig` (same name replaces, else the first empty slot) instead of DFU-writing bank slot 0. Rigs never go over DFU
- **Pedal knobs hold still**: `ControlBuffer` adds a 0.15%-of-travel hysteresis, so an untouched knob reads exactly the same every poll and the parameters it drives settle (a jittering knob kept a reverb rig about 8 points busier); after a slot, mode or rig change a knob takes over once moved 2% of its travel, instead of soft takeover's pickup
- **One plugin bundle**: all plugins ship in a single `sonido.clap` (one CLAP factory, 37 plugins) and one `sonido.vst3` shim, replacing a file per effect. One release link instead of one per plugin; plugin IDs are unchanged, so saved projects still load. `make plugins` installs `~/.clap/sonido.clap`; `make plugins-vst3` also installs `~/.vst3/sonido.vst3`. Old per-effect `sonido-*.clap`/`.vst3` installs can be deleted
- **Plugins no longer link ALSA**: `sonido-plugin` dropped an unused `sonido-io` dependency. CI no longer installs `libudev-dev`, which nothing used
- **Editor effect panels** use the plugin cabinet's controls (LED-ring knobs with value and label under each ring, segmented/stepper selectors) in the effect's family color, and the header carries the vector SONIDO wordmark
- **`make plugins` install names**: installed CLAPs now use the dashed bundle names (`sonido-delay.clap`, previously `sonido_delay.clap`), matching `dist-clap/` and release artifacts — required so the VST3 shims can pair by filename stem. Old underscore installs can be deleted
- **Dependency health**: `cargo deny check` passes clean — deny.toml moved to the cargo-deny v2 schema, `anyhow` and `crossbeam-epoch` bumped past RUSTSEC-2026-0190/0204, the two quick-xml advisories documented as non-actionable transitives, and the git sources (clack, baseview) pinned in an explicit allow list

### Removed
- `sonido_config::factory_patches` (the graph player lists `sonido_rigs::factory_rigs`; TOML factory presets remain for the CLI), the pedal's three compiled-in presets (replaced by the factory rigs), `sonido-daisy`'s unused `src/qspi.rs` preset-sector layout, and `examples/qspi_read_test.rs`, replaced by `examples/qspi_test.rs` (JEDEC id, write guard, state-sector write/verify/erase, slot 0 decode)
- **The CLI's legacy `sonido daisy` command** and its position-indexed `SOND` sector format; `sonido pedal` and `sonido patch` replace it
- **`sonido-gui`'s `dfu` module** (moved to `sonido_patch::dfu`, firmware only) and DFU rig flashing
- **Pedal gestures**: footswitch effect scrolling, the FS1 double-tap clear, both-footswitch bypass, and the 3 s both-footswitch bootloader hold

### Fixed
- **Pedal: every rig change dropped audio** three or four times: one executor ran audio and everything else, and building a rig took up to 12 ms (a factory rig's graph) against about 0.6 ms of slack. The audio task now runs on its own interrupt-priority executor and preempts every other task; the rest reach the pedal's state through a short critical section. The cabinet also built its impulse response a second time in its first audio block (~4 ms of software trig); it now normalizes with a rotating phasor and keeps the IR it was built with. Measured: 0 codec overruns across every factory rig, the jam, and USB flash writes.
